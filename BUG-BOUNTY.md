# The Household Bug Bounty

House rule: **if you can beat the network, you win, as long as you play fair.**

This network limits screen time and blocks some sites. You are allowed, even
encouraged, to try to get around it. But there's a deal:

## The deal (responsible disclosure)

1. Find a way around a block or a time limit.
2. **Don't just quietly use it.** Come and *show Dad how you did it.*
3. You get a reward (below), and we fix it together.
4. Quietly abusing a hole you found = the hole gets closed AND you lose the
   bounty. Reporting it = you get paid. Same as how real security works.

This is exactly how professional "bug bounties" work: companies pay hackers
to find holes and report them instead of exploiting them. You're doing the
real thing.

## The levels (each one teaches a real skill)

| Lvl | Challenge | What you'll learn | Bounty |
|----|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| 1 | Change your DNS to 8.8.8.8 and still get blocked? Figure out why. | What DNS is | 15 min |
| 2 | Turn on "Secure DNS" / DoH in your browser, or Private DNS on Android (DoT), and beat the filter | Encrypted DNS (DoH/DoT) | 30 min |
| 3 | Give your device a static IP to dodge a block | DHCP vs static IP | 30 min |
| 4 | Change (spoof) your device's MAC address | What a MAC address is | 45 min |
| 5 | Tunnel out with a VPN or your own hotspot (BOSS LEVEL) | VPNs, tunnelling, NAT | 60 min + bragging rights |

Reward can be screen-time minutes or cash, agree it with Dad first.

## Rules

- No breaking other people's stuff. No touching Dad's work network (you
  can't reach it anyway, and trying isn't the game).
- Show your working. "It just worked" doesn't count; explain *how*.
- Every hole you find makes this project better for other families too,
  it's open source. You're on the pentest team now.
